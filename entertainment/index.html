Topics in Entertainment

Entertainment on Liora Social is where fandom lives. A Topic might ask for your favourite character redrawn in your own style, the last film that surprised you, or a manga panel you think everyone should see. Answers from every corner of the fandom end up in the same gallery.

Projects give a fandom a home: a page with its own banner and colour, Topics for art, discussion and recommendations, and a chat where the conversation continues between posts.

Entertainment on Liora, answered

What counts as Entertainment on Liora?
Films, series, anime, comics and manga, music, books and games as fandoms. Fan art, scene picks, recommendations and discussion all belong here.
Can I share fan art on Liora?
Yes. Fan art is one of the most common things posted to Entertainment Topics. Manga Panels and Frame by Frame are two Projects built around it.
Is Liora a place to discuss shows and films?
Yes. Post to a Topic with a photo or a few lines, and continue in the Project’s Chat tab, where the conversation is not tied to a single post.
Can I start a Project for my fandom?
Yes. Create a Project, give it a banner and a colour, add Topics for art, discussion and recommendations, and invite people to join.
