A social app for the games you play and make.

Post environment designs, character art, devlogs and screenshots to gaming Topics. Join a Project like an indie game in progress, and watch it grow update by update.

Topics in Gaming

Gaming on Liora Social is not a feed of clips fighting for attention. It is a set of Topics: “share some of your favorite game environment designs”, “post the character you are working on”, “a screenshot from the last hour you played”. You post to the Topic that fits, and the people into the same thing see it.

Game makers use Projects as a public devlog. Devin’s Video Game Development is one: a retro-style arcade game with its own Topics for environment design and character art, a chat, and a growing list of members who follow the build.

Gaming on Liora, answered

Is Liora a social app for gamers?
Yes. Liora Social has gaming Topics for environment design, character art, devlogs and screenshots, and Projects where a game and its community live in one place.
Can I use Liora as a devlog for my indie game?
Yes. Create a Project for your game, add Topics for the parts you want feedback on, and post as you build. Members can join, follow along and chat with you.
What kind of gaming content works on Liora?
Environment and level design, character concepts, pixel art, UI mockups, screenshots and short written updates. Liora is built for images and short text, not for streaming.
Is there a place to talk, not just post?
Every Topic and Project has a Chat tab. That is where people ask questions, share progress and organise around the game.
